Version 3.0 supersedes version 2.2. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR) unless stated.
What changed in version 3.0 (effective 1 July 2026)
• Meal limits revised for all grades (Section 4).
• Temporary accommodation on relocation reduced to 10 nights (Section 6.4).
• Household goods shifting limit increased to INR 60,000 (Section 6.5).
1. Purpose and Scope
1.1 This policy covers business travel, expense claims, relocation benefits for internal transfers, travel advances,
full and final settlement and corporate cards.
1.2 It applies to all permanent employees. Grades follow the HR Policy Handbook, Section 1.4.
1.3 Where this policy and the HR Policy Handbook differ on a financial benefit, this policy prevails.
2. Expense Claims
2.1 Submit claims on the finance portal within 30 days of the trip end date or expense date.
2.2 Claims submitted more than 60 days after the trip end date or expense date are rejected.
2.3 Original receipts are required for any single item above INR 500.
2.4 Claims approved by the 20th of a month are paid with that month's salary. Claims approved after the 20th are
paid with the following month's salary.
3. Travel Class
3.1 Domestic flights: economy class for all grades.
3.2 International flights: economy class; G6 to G7 may fly business class on flights longer than 6 hours.
3.3 Trains: AC 2-tier for G1 to G5; AC First Class for G6 to G7.
3.4 Book through the travel desk. Self-booked tickets are reimbursed only if cheaper than the travel desk fare.
4. Daily Limits
4.1 Metro cities are Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Pune. All other cities are
non-metro.

and USD 120 for G6 to G7.
5. Approvals
5.1 Claims up to INR 25,000 are approved by the reporting manager.
5.2 Claims above INR 25,000 need approval from both the reporting manager and the department head.
5.3 International travel needs pre-approval from the department head before booking.

6. Relocation Benefits for Internal Transfers
6.1 Eligibility is as defined in the HR Policy Handbook, Section 4.1.
6.2 One-time relocation allowance, paid with the first salary at the new location and taxable as per law:
Grade Relocation allowance
G1 to G3 INR 40,000
G4 to G5 INR 75,000
G6 to G7 INR 1,25,000
6.3 Travel to the new location: one-way economy flight or AC train for the employee and dependents (spouse
and up to 2 children).
6.4 Temporary accommodation: up to 10 nights at the employee's grade hotel limit (Section 4). This limit replaces
any earlier temporary accommodation limit stated in other company policies, effective 1 July 2026.
6.5 Household goods shifting: reimbursed up to INR 60,000, based on three quotations from company-approved
movers. The lowest quotation is reimbursed.
6.6 Recovery: if the employee resigns within 12 months of the relocation date, the relocation allowance (Section
6.2) is recovered in proportion to the unserved months of the 12-month period. Example: an employee who
resigns after 9 months repays 3/12 of the allowance.
7. Travel Advances
7.1 A travel advance of up to 80% of the estimated trip cost may be requested.
7.2 Advances must be settled within 15 days of return. Unsettled advances are deducted from salary.
8. Full and Final Settlement
8.1 The full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
8.2 It requires the IT asset clearance certificate (IT Policy Section 8.3) and clearance from the reporting
manager.
8.3 It includes salary for days worked and earned leave encashment (HR Policy Handbook, Section 3.1), minus
recoveries such as notice period shortfall, relocation recovery (Section 6.6) and unsettled advances (Section 7.2).
9. Corporate Cards
9.1 Corporate cards are issued to G5 and above.
9.2 Cardholders reconcile their monthly statement on the finance portal by the 5th of the following month.
