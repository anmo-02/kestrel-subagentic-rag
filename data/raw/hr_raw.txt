This handbook applies to all permanent employees. Financial benefits referenced here are defined in the Travel, Expense &
Relocation Policy (KSPL-FIN-POL-003). IT equipment and access rules are defined in the IT Services & Information Security Policy
(KSPL-IT-POL-002).
1. Purpose and Scope
1.1 This handbook applies to all permanent employees of Kestrel Systems Pvt. Ltd. working from the Pune
(headquarters), Mumbai and Bengaluru offices.
1.2 Contract staff and interns are covered by the terms of their individual contracts and are not covered by this
handbook unless stated.
1.3 Where this handbook refers to another company policy for a topic, the referenced policy governs that topic.
1.4 Employee grades: G1 to G3 are Associate levels, G4 to G5 are Manager levels, and G6 to G7 are Senior
Leadership levels.
2. Working Hours and Hybrid Work
2.1 The standard work week is Monday to Friday, 40 hours. Core hours, when every employee must be
available, are 11:00 to 16:00 IST.
2.2 Hybrid work: employees must work from their assigned office for a minimum of 3 days per week.
2.3 Working from another city in India: employees may work from another city in India for up to 20 working days
per calendar year with reporting manager approval, requested on the HR portal at least 5 working days in
advance. More than 20 working days in a calendar year requires additional approval from the HR Business
Partner.
2.4 Working from outside India is not part of the hybrid allowance. It requires written approval from both the HR
Business Partner and IT Security (see IT Policy Section 4.4) and is limited to 15 working days per calendar year.
2.5 Employees working away from the office must connect to company systems only through the company VPN,
as described in IT Policy Section 4.

4. Internal Transfer and Relocation
4.1 Eligibility: an employee must have completed at least 12 months of continuous service on the transfer date. A
transfer may be initiated by the company, or requested by the employee and approved by both the current and
the receiving department heads.
4.2 Process: the employee submits Form HR-12 (Relocation Request) on the HR portal at least 30 days before
the planned move date. HR confirms the transfer within 7 working days.
4.3 Benefits: the employee receives 3 paid working days of relocation leave. Financial benefits, including the
relocation allowance, travel to the new location and household goods shifting, are defined in the Travel, Expense
& Relocation Policy, Section 6.
4.4 Temporary accommodation: on arrival at the new location, the employee may stay in a company-arranged
guest house or hotel for up to 15 nights.
4.5 IT equipment during relocation is handled as per IT Policy Section 6.
4.6 An employee who resigns within 12 months of relocating may have to repay part of the relocation allowance,
as per the Travel, Expense & Relocation Policy, Section 6.6.
5. Onboarding
5.1 New joiners must submit the following on or before the first working day:
• PAN card and Aadhaar card
• Cancelled cheque or bank statement showing account details
• Highest education certificates
• Relieving letter from the previous employer
• Two passport-size photographs
5.2 HR raises the account and laptop request to IT at least 5 working days before the joining date (see IT Policy
Section 3.1).
5.3 Induction runs on the first two working days. Every new joiner must acknowledge this handbook on the HR
portal within 7 days of joining.
5.4 Probation lasts 6 months. HR issues a confirmation letter on successful completion.
6. Resignation and Exit
6.1 Notice periods after confirmation:
Grade Notice period
G1 to G3 30 days
G4 to G5 60 days
G6 to G7 90 days
Any grade during probation 15 days
6.2 Notice buyout: with manager approval, an employee may buy out the unserved notice period by paying basic
salary for the unserved days. The company may also waive notice.
6.3 Exit clearance: the employee returns the laptop, access card and all other company assets on the last
working day (see IT Policy Section 8).
6.4 Full and final settlement is processed as per the Travel, Expense & Relocation Policy, Section 8.
6.5 The experience letter is issued within 7 days of the full and final settlement.
